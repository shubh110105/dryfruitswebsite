Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do you deliver across all of Lucknow?
+
Yes! We deliver to all areas of Lucknow — Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj, Aliganj, Indira Nagar, Alambagh, and more. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12 PM.
How fresh are your dry fruits?
+
Extremely fresh! We source directly from farms and pack in small batches every week. Glass jars maintain freshness for 3–6 months. No preservatives, no compromises.
Can I customize the labels for corporate orders?
+
Absolutely! We offer fully custom labels with your company logo, message, and brand colors. Minimum order for custom branding is 50 jars. Turnaround is 48–72 hours.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), bank transfer, and Cash on Delivery for orders under ₹2,000. Corporate orders can also be fulfilled via NEFT/cheque with invoice.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
+
No minimum for retail orders — buy just 1 jar! For corporate orders with custom branding, minimum is 50 units. Bulk pricing starts at 20+ jars of the same SKU.
Do you offer subscription boxes?
+
Yes! Our monthly subscription box delivers a curated selection of 3 premium jars to your doorstep every month. Subscribe and save 15% automatically. WhatsApp us to enroll!
